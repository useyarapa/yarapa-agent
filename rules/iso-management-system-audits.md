# ISO Management-System Audits

Use this profile with [`compliance-audit-scope-and-standards.md`](compliance-audit-scope-and-standards.md), [`compliance-audit-evidence.md`](compliance-audit-evidence.md), and [`compliance-audit-findings-and-reporting.md`](compliance-audit-findings-and-reporting.md) when auditing an ISO management-system or related guidance standard.

## Check the edition before each assessment

The editions below were checked against ISO pages on 2026-09-29. They are navigation aids, not a lasting baseline. Recheck each official page for publication status, amendments, withdrawal, and transition arrangements before an audit. Use the licensed standard as the normative source.

| Framework | Edition checked | Role and profile |
|---|---|---|
| [ISO/IEC 27001](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) | 2022 + Amd 1:2024 | ISMS requirements. The amendment concerns climate-action changes; verify the official amended text. |
| [ISO/IEC 27002](https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html) | 2022 | Information-security control guidance; it is not independently certifiable. |
| [ISO/IEC 27701](https://www.iso.org/standard/27701) | 2025 | Privacy information management system requirements and guidance; ISO lists the 2019 edition as withdrawn. |
| [ISO 22301](https://www.iso.org/standard/75106.html) | 2019 + Amd 1:2024 | Business continuity management system requirements. ISO lists a replacement as under development; use the current published edition, not a draft, unless a draft review is requested. |
| [ISO/IEC 42001](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) | 2023 | AI management system requirements. |
| [ISO/IEC 20000-1](https://www.iso.org/standard/70636.html) | 2018 + Amd 1:2024 | Service management system requirements. |
| [ISO 31000](https://www.iso.org/standard/65694.html) | 2018 | Risk-management guidance; it is not a certifiable management-system requirements standard. ISO indicates revision activity, so check status before use. |
| [ISO 9001](https://www.iso.org/standard/9001) | 2026 | Quality management system requirements. For organizations certified to the prior edition, verify transition arrangements with the relevant certification body. |

## Apply the relevant scope gate

- **ISO/IEC 27001:** establish ISMS boundaries, context and interested parties, information assets/processes/services, risk assessment and treatment method, applicable obligations, control-applicability record (such as the Statement of Applicability), and evidence period. Assess leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, internal audit, management review, corrective action, and improvement. For each applicable control, record identifier, applicability rationale, owner, design and implementation evidence, recurring operating samples, exceptions, and compensating arrangements. Group controls by organizational, people, physical, or technological themes when useful.
- **ISO/IEC 27002:** report alignment with guidance or control implementation; do not report certification or conformity to 27002.
- **ISO/IEC 27701:** establish PIMS scope, controller/processor roles, PII and data-subject categories, purposes and flows, privacy obligations, processors and transfers, retention/deletion, data-subject request mechanisms, risk assessment, and operating evidence.
- **ISO 22301:** establish BCMS scope, critical products/services/processes, business-impact analysis, disruption scenarios and dependencies, continuity strategies, recovery objectives, plans, exercises, communications, management review, and improvement.
- **ISO/IEC 42001:** establish AIMS scope, AI system inventory and roles, intended use and affected stakeholders, risks and impacts, lifecycle and supplier dependencies, data/model governance evidence where applicable, human oversight, transparency, monitoring, incident/change handling, objectives, audits, and corrective actions. Match evidence to the specific AI use; policy alone does not establish system safety or effectiveness.
- **ISO/IEC 20000-1:** establish service-management scope, service catalog and customers, requirements and service levels, supplier relationships, change/release/configuration/incident/problem processes, availability, capacity, continuity, security interfaces, monitoring, audit, and improvement.
- **ISO 31000:** evaluate governance and integration, context and criteria, risk identification/analysis/evaluation/treatment, communication, monitoring, recording, and evidence that risk treatment affects decisions. Report alignment with guidance, not certification.
- **ISO 9001:** establish QMS scope, products/services, customer and applicable statutory/regulatory requirements, process ownership, objectives and measures, competence/resources, design where applicable, external providers, production/service controls, nonconforming outputs, customer feedback, audit, management review, corrective action, and improvement.

Repository or technical evidence can establish specific technical facts. It cannot by itself prove organization-wide conformity or certification to an ISO management-system standard.
